What Pro adds

Protection for the work you have already put in

The free card stays free. Pro is the safety net and the finishing layer on top of it.

Automatic daily backups

Every card on every dashboard is backed up each day and kept for a rolling 30 days. Break something at 2 AM and roll back in seconds.

Named snapshots

Save up to 30 named versions per card. Seasonal layouts, safe experiments, milestones before a big change. Restore any of them with one click.

Cloud sync across devices

Edit on the desktop, check the tablet, glance at your phone. Every device stays in step through your ultracard.io account.

One-click export and import

Export a whole card as a single compressed file. Share it, keep a local copy, or import a layout from the community and make it yours.

Web dashboard

Browse backups and snapshots, restore versions, manage billing and invoices from any browser at ultracard.io.

Priority support

A private channel on the Ultra Card Discord where Pro members get answers first, directly from the developer.

Setup

Up and running in about two minutes

1

Choose a plan

Pick Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime in the Billing Cycle box above. Your ultracard.io account is created at checkout if you do not have one yet.

2

Install the cloud integration

In Home Assistant, add Ultra Card Pro Cloud from HACS, then sign in once with your ultracard.io login.

3

Pro unlocks everywhere

Every device on your Home Assistant instance gets Pro automatically. No per-device login, and the token refreshes itself.
